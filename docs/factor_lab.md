# Factor Lab

The factor lab evaluates candidate signals before they influence strategy decisions.

Implemented metrics:

- Rank IC
- ICIR
- quantile spread
- turnover
- t-stat
- p-value
- Benjamini-Hochberg q-value
- monotonicity flag

The miner writes accepted/rejected candidate outputs with explicit rejection reasons. Candidate factors are not blessed just because an agent proposes them.
